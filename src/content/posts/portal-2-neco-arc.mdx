### Information

One of the great joys of the internet is cats.  
One of the *greater* joys of the internet is the funny cat.

This mod replaces Wheatley's in-game model with a model of Neco-Arc (created by [Paper Bandit](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/neco-arc-8bcd385adec44fdf8ebfc63bcdf5b28c)).
