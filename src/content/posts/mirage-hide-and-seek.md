> [!IMPORTANT]
> This map does not work while running the game in Counter-Strike 2 mode. \
> To play the map, you need to run in CS:GO mode, by enabling it through Steam Beta.

### Information

This is an edit of the map de_mirage by Valve, I did not make the original map.

Max Players:  
16 T – 16 CT

Timings:  
T – 1 Minute to hide  
CT – 6 Minutes to seek

An edit of de_mirage with more props, less invisible walls, access to most roofs and added secrets (hidden doors and breakable walls).

### Requirements

You’ll need to own the game to use this map. [Get it here (store.steampowered.com)](https://store.steampowered.com/app/730/)
